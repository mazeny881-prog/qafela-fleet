About the System

Qafela is an advanced, comprehensive system designed specifically for managing heavy transportation fleets, tracking routes, monitoring heavy trucks and trailer trips, managing drivers, and recording maintenance logs and breakdowns with high precision to ensure operational efficiency and provide real-time follow-up reports.
